# Market Research & Industry Landscape

## 1. Industry Context
Enterprise attack surfaces have expanded exponentially across hybrid cloud architectures, SaaS stacks, and distributed microservices. Legacy standalone cybersecurity point solutions force SOC teams into context-switching fatigue, missed alerts, and delayed MTTR.

## 2. Market Sizing & Trends
- Enterprise Cybersecurity Market: Expected to exceed $300B by 2028.
- Agentic AI & SOAR Market: Projected CAGR of 18.5% through 2030.
- Key Drivers: Shortage of specialized tier-1 and tier-2 analysts, proliferation of automated attacks, and the emergence of autonomous AI threats.

## 3. Target Demographics
- **SMEs**: Turnkey enterprise-grade protection without massive SOC headcount.
- **Enterprise SOCs**: Streamlined triage, automated correlation, and explainable AI insights.
- **MSSPs**: Multi-tenant visibility, automated investigation workflows, and standardized SLA reporting.
